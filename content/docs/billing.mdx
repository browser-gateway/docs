Applies to `app.browsergateway.com` (cloud). The OSS `browser-gateway` npm package is free forever with no billing.

## The model in one paragraph

No subscriptions. You start free. Everything that costs us real money (session recording, observability broadcast, BYOK usage above the ceiling) draws from your wallet at a published per-unit rate. When you want more, you top up. The amount you top up is exactly the amount added to your wallet: we absorb the card processing cost. Our revenue comes from margin on the metered features you use. Your browser provider bill (Browserless, Steel, Browserbase, Cloudflare Browser Rendering, or your own runtime) is separate.

## Account states

| State | How you get here |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Signed up, no top-up yet. Uses free features up to fair-use limits. |
| **Paid** | You have credit balance greater than zero from a top-up. |
| **Enterprise** | You have a contract with us. Higher ceilings, custom retention, SSO, SLAs. |

You do NOT need to be on a specific "plan" to use the product. Every account gets free BYOK routing. Metered features unlock when your wallet has balance.

## What every account gets for free

Free without any wallet balance:

- **BYOK routing**: 100,000 sessions per month included on your own browser providers.
- **Bandwidth**: unlimited, no meter.
- **All routing strategies**: priority-chain, round-robin, least-connections, weighted, latency-optimized.
- **Multi-provider failover**.
- **Live playground**.
- **Up to 100 saved profiles**, 10 MB per profile, 500 MB total per workspace.
- **60-day session log retention**.
- **100 sessions per day** rate limit.

A zero-balance wallet does not unlock session recording, observability broadcast to external tools, or the account REST API.

## What a positive wallet balance unlocks

Anything below requires wallet balance greater than zero. Charges draw at session close:

- **Session recording** at `$0.007` per minute of encoded video, drawn from wallet.
- **Observability broadcast** at `$0.05` per 1,000 events, drawn from wallet.
- **[Data API](/cloud-data-api)** at `$0.001` per successful call (screenshot / content / scrape).
- **10,000 sessions per day** rate limit (up from 100).
- **[Cloud REST API](/cloud-rest-api)** with a raised daily cap (100,000 calls/day up from 5,000).
- BYOK sessions above the 100k monthly ceiling at `$0.0005` per session, drawn from wallet.

## What Enterprise adds

Enterprise is a contract, not a self-serve plan:

- Raised BYOK ceiling (default 1M sessions per month, custom above).
- Volume-based discounts on metered rates (recording, observability broadcast, BYOK overage).
- SSO / SAML.
- Admin controls.
- Custom retention: 60d, 90d, 180d, or 365d for session logs, replays, and observability.
- Contractual SLAs.
- Shared Slack support channel.

## Rate card

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| BYOK routing | Free up to 100,000 sessions/mo, `$0.0005`/session above |
| Bandwidth | Free, unlimited |
| Session recording | `$0.007` per minute of encoded video |
| Observability broadcast | `$0.05` per 1,000 events |
| Data API (screenshot / content / scrape) | `$0.001` per successful call |
| Wallet top-up fee | None. You pay what you top up. |
| Minimum top-up | `$5` |
| Maximum top-up | `$25,000` per transaction |

## Wallet mechanics

- Wallet balance is denominated in US dollars.
- Purchased balance carries across periods.
- No signup credit.
- Refunds available within 24 hours of transaction, unused balance only.
- Payments processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

## Retention

Flat for all accounts. Enterprise contracts can extend.

| Resource | Retention |
|---|---|
| Session logs | 60 days |
| Session recordings | 30 days |
| Observability event traces | 60 days |
| Profiles | Kept indefinitely |

## When your wallet hits zero

- Recording and observability broadcast requests return HTTP 402 at CDP connect time.
- BYOK routing continues free below the 100k ceiling.
- BYOK routing above the ceiling returns HTTP 402 until you top up.
- All other free features stay available.

Recordings and observability captures that were already in progress when the balance hit zero stop cleanly. The session itself keeps running.
